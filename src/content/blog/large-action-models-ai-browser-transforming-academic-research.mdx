## Large Action Models

### What Are LAMs

[Large action models](https://fellou.ai/blog/philosophical-shift-agentic-browser-impact-ai-browser/) are a new kind of ai model. These models help with tasks, not just making text. A large action model knows what someone wants to do. It listens to what you say and then acts to finish the job. For example, it can fill out forms, get data, or sort files. This makes large action models helpful for academic research. They help by doing boring jobs for researchers. The model can use many websites and tools. It can also work with files on your computer. This is possible because it understands context well. The model learns from what people do and changes to fit new jobs. Large action models do more than answer questions. They are helpers that finish tasks.

### LAMs vs LLMs

Many people have heard of large language models. These models make text by guessing the next word. They help with writing, making summaries, or answering questions. But large action models do even more. They act like agents that decide and finish tasks. The [table below shows how they are different](https://medium.com/version-1/the-rise-of-large-action-models-lams-how-ai-can-understand-and-execute-human-intentions-f59c8e78bc09):

| Aspect | Large Language Models (LLMs) | Large Action Models (LAMs) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Function | Make text by guessing next words or tokens | Work as agents that do tasks and make choices |
| Interaction with Environment | Only use text for input and output | Connect with other systems to do real actions |
| Autonomy | Tools that just make language | Helpers that can do tasks right away |
| Understanding Human Intent | Focus on making good text | Know what people want and turn it into actions |
| Adaptability | Only make text | Change to fit new things and talk with people |
| Application Domains | Mostly used for language jobs | Used for many things, like research |

[Large action models](https://fellou.ai/blog/agentic-browser-fellou-next-evolution-beyond-ai-browsers/) are a big improvement. They go from just making text to doing real tasks. This makes them better for busy and task-based research work.

## AI Browser Evolution

### From Chat to Action

Old AI browsers were like talking helpers. They could answer questions and help you search for things. But they could not do tasks for you. You had to open new tabs and copy things yourself. You also had to move files on your own. The AI in these browsers only worked with words, not actions.

Agentic browsers are different. They do more than just chat. These browsers use smart AI to know what you want to do. The AI can [do things like find data, fill out forms, and sort files](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/goodness-agentic-browsers-here-shocking-dr-amanda-j-williamson-brzsc). For example, an agentic browser can get research articles, pick out main ideas, and save them in a folder with one command. Now, the browser does the work, not just talk.

### Agentic Browsers

Agentic browsers have [AI agents inside the browser](https://www.youreverydayai.com/agentic-ai-in-the-browser-the-next-frontier-of-artificial-intelligence/). These agents work with your files and web apps without extra help. The AI can handle hard jobs, like mixing data from many places or planning tasks on different sites.

Some agentic browsers can go to both public and locked sites. They can gather info and make reports by themselves. They use your device to process data, so they are faster and keep things private. You do not have to log in again and again. The model can remember what tabs you had open and your browsing history. This helps make research tasks easier.

[Agentic browsers make research](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-agentic-workflows-productivity-automation/) faster and fit your needs. They cut down on boring work and make it easier to learn. Studies show groups using agentic AI tools are [25% happier and 30% more productive](https://superagi.com/agentic-ai-vs-traditional-ai-a-comparative-analysis-of-capabilities-and-applications/). The AI browser is now a real research helper. It lets you spend more time on ideas and less on repeating tasks.

## LAMs and AI in Research

### Workflow Automation

Large action models and [AI browsers](https://fellou.ai/) have changed how researchers work. They help by [doing many research steps automatically](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12089777/). For example, these tools can check abstracts, pull out data, and write parts of a paper. Researchers do not need to do every step themselves. The AI can do boring jobs, like sorting files or filling out forms. This saves time and helps stop mistakes.

Researchers notice big changes after using large action models. They [get more done because the AI can do many things at once](https://www.akira.ai/blog/transforming-llms-into-large-action-model). The models also make fewer mistakes than people. Teams can finish more work without hiring more people. Costs go down because less hand work is needed. The AI can also work with other systems, so hard jobs are easier. These changes let researchers spend more time on new ideas and making good choices.

> Tip: Letting AI do boring jobs gives researchers more time to think and less time doing the same thing over and over.

### Data and Knowledge Management

Academic research makes a lot of data. Large action models help keep this data in order. They can get data from many places, sort it, and keep it updated. For example, in reviews, the AI can pull out important facts from many studies. It can also check for mistakes and make sure the data is right.

Some projects [use large language models to get data from clinical trial reports](https://doi.org/10.1002/jrsm.1710). These models can work with many kinds of data, like trial results, patient groups, and outcomes. They use [smart prompts to check each other's work](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11469465/), which helps them be more correct. This way, researchers do not need to make big training sets for every new job. The AI can learn and change fast.

Researchers now use data to help make choices more often. The AI helps them find patterns and understand big sets of data. This leads to better choices and stronger research.

### Collaboration

Working together is very important in research. Large action models and AI browsers help teams in new ways. They let many AI agents work together on one project. Each agent can do a different job. For example, one agent can find news, another can look at data, and a third can check for risks.

In some stories, teams used AI agents to finish hard projects faster. One group got answers in minutes instead of hours by [letting agents scan news, study data, and check risks](https://dr-arsanjani.medium.com/multi-agent-sofwtare-engineering-orchestrating-the-future-of-ai-in-financial-services-part-2-d14cee8a4d54). Another team used agents to watch for risks in real time, saving time and money. These stories show how AI helps teams work faster and smarter.

Researchers can now use AI to handle jobs, share what they know, and solve problems together. The [AI browser](https://fellou.ai/) is a place for all these things. It brings people, data, and tools together. This makes working together easier and better.

## Challenges for AI in Academia

![Challenges for AI in Academia](https://statics.mylandingpages.co/static/aaanxdmf26c522mpaaagiwvqxeocaqpo/image/7491e6d40e8b483f87375758e91c57b4.webp)

### Privacy and Security

Academic researchers worry about [privacy and security](https://education.illinois.edu/about/news-events/news/article/2024/10/24/ai-in-schools--pros-and-cons) with ai tools. They want to know how their data is used and kept safe. Many are afraid of leaks or someone using their data the wrong way. This is a big worry when the research is private or has personal details. Some people also think ai could make cheating or copying easier. To help with these worries, some ai browsers have privacy modes. These modes let users stop data collection and keep things private. User control lets researchers pick what the ai can see. This helps them feel safer using these tools.

> Note: Good privacy settings and user control help researchers trust ai tools more.

### Reliability

Reliability is a big problem for ai in research. [Studies show large language models sometimes give wrong answers](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07930-y). They can sound sure even when they are wrong. These models can mess up hard tasks, just like people do. But they do not always know when to stop or ask for help. This can cause mistakes in research results. [Schools use different ways to check if ai is right](https://www.sourcely.net/resources/top-10-ai-tools-for-ensuring-content-credibility-and-accuracy). They use tools to check sources, look for copying, and review where info comes from. Experts also check what the ai finds to make sure it is right. Using ai with human checks makes research more reliable.

### Adoption

It can be hard for schools to start using ai tools. Some problems are high costs, not enough training, and worries about changing old ways of teaching. Many researchers are afraid of losing close ties with students or coworkers. Schools try different things to help people use ai. [They run test programs, teach teachers and students, and work with tech partners](https://internet2.edu/a-new-phase-of-ai-adoption-in-higher-education/). They also look for new money and build support for ai use. These steps help more people try ai and see how it helps. When schools plan well and help users, more people start using ai tools.

Large action models and AI browsers help researchers work faster. These tools do jobs like sorting data and handling files. Teams use ai to spot new patterns and get better results. [Agentic ai](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-vs-agentic-browser-assisting-vs-doing/) gives special help, like making work steps automatic and helping teams work together. Many experts think ai will be important in research. Readers can see how these tools could change their own work.

## FAQ

### What is the main difference between a large action model and a large language model?

A large action model does jobs in apps and websites. A large language model only makes text or answers questions. The action model helps with real tasks, not just words.

### How does an AI browser help academic researchers?

An [AI browser](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-simplifies-research-reporting-for-academics/) does research steps for you. It can get data, sort files, and handle tasks. Researchers save time and do not repeat the same work. The browser also lets teams share and work together.

### Can AI browsers keep research data private?

Many AI browsers have privacy modes. These modes stop data from being collected and keep user info safe. Researchers can pick what the AI can see. This helps keep research private.

### What tasks can large action models automate in research?

[Large action models](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-consulting-tasks-automation-save-time-productivity/) can check articles, pull out data, fill forms, and sort files. They also help with planning and making reports. Researchers use them to finish simple jobs fast.
