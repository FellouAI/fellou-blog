## Best Practices for AI Screening in Hiring

### Data Privacy and Security

You need to keep candidate data safe when using ai screening. There are risks like someone using data without permission or leaking it. Sometimes, bad data can even change the results. You should [use safe data channels and encrypt important information](https://www.alston.com/en/insights/publications/2025/06/joint-guidance-ai-data-security). Always check where your data comes from and use trusted sources only. Give access to candidate data only to people who need it. Do regular checks and privacy reviews to find problems early. Let candidates know how their data is used and stored. This helps build trust and keeps your ai screening safe.

> Tip: Turn on privacy modes and use safe storage to protect candidate data and follow rules like GDPR.

### Bias Mitigation

You want ai screening to treat everyone fairly. Bias can happen if you do not watch out for it. Remove names, photos, and other personal details from resumes. This lets ai look at skills and experience only. Use the same skill tests for every candidate. Fairness-aware algorithms can help find hidden bias and fix it. Check your ai screening tool often to spot and fix bias. For example, [Unilever used hidden profiles and got more women in management](https://www.mokahr.io/myblog/ai-diversity-hiring-reduce-bias/). [Collect and look at data to see if there are any bias patterns](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-023-02079-x).

*   Ways to lower bias:
    
    *   Hide names and photos before screening.
        
    *   Use skill tests for everyone.
        
    *   Check your ai screening tool often.
        
    *   Train ai with different types of data.
        

### Human Oversight

You should [always have a person check ai screening](https://www.bsr.org/en/emerging-issues/ai-in-hiring). AI can help you go faster, but people still need to make big choices. Review ai decisions, especially before hiring someone. Human checks help catch mistakes and keep things fair. Tell candidates when you use ai and let them ask questions or say no. Recruiters should look at ai suggestions and make sure they fit your company’s values. Mixing ai and people gives better hiring and a good experience for everyone.

| Best Practice | Supporting Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Use AI to reduce human bias and increase efficiency | AI screening does better than people by at least 25%. L'Oreal cut resume review time from 40 to 4 minutes. |
| Combine AI screening with post-screening assessments | Fun tests and AI-reviewed interviews help find good hires and keep candidates interested. |
| Ensure positive candidate experience, especially for rejected applicants | Good rejection experiences make people want to apply again and say nice things. |
| Maintain human oversight over AI decisions | Recruiters say people should make the final choice to build trust and lower bias. |
| Continuously optimize AI algorithms | Training and checking ai often makes it more fair and correct. |
| Promote transparency and candidate control | Candidates like clear steps and want to control their own data. |

> Note: When you use ai screening and people together, you hire faster, fairer, and get better results.
