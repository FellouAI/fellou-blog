# A Quick Tour of What Fellou Can Do

## Fellou Possesses Cross-Platform Deep Search Capabilities

Fellou supports deep search, enabling parallel, batch searches across both public web content and login-required platforms like Quora, X, and LinkedIn. This drastically speeds up research by retrieving more comprehensive information at once, and lets you generate shareable visual reports with a single click.

## Fellou Can Perform Cross-Webpage Operational Tasks

Fellou can automatically plan tasks, invoke tools, and execute actions to coordinate operations across multiple web interfaces, enabling various in-browser tasks. These include shopping, scheduling meetings, sending emails, and posting tweets based on webpage content.

## Fellou Enables Asynchronous Collaboration with Users

Fellou supports a tag-group feature. While the Fellou Agent executes assigned tasks, users can switch tag groups at the bottom to continue working on other tasks. Once Fellou completes its task, it will notify users, helping your work more efficiently.
