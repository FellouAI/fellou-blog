### Privacy and Human Touch

AI brings many benefits, but you still need to watch out for some challenges. [Privacy is a big deal in HR](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/all-things-work/ai-hr-challenges-opportunities). Regular checks and audits help you spot problems early.

Here’s a quick look at what you should keep in mind:

| Challenge | What You Can Do |
| --- | --- |

| Bias | Check AI results and use fair data |

| Human Oversight | Always review important decisions |

You also need to keep the human touch. AI helps with training and performance management, but you know your team best. Stay involved, give feedback, and use AI as a tool—not a replacement for your judgment.

## FAQ

### What is an AI browser, and how does it help in HR?

An AI browser is a smart tool that does tasks for you online. You can use it to post jobs, check resumes, and manage files. It saves you time and helps you work better.

### Can I trust AI browsers with private employee data?

Yes, you can. AI browsers use strong security to [keep your data safe](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-security-compliance-client-data-privacy-consultants/). You control who sees the information. Always check your settings and review who has access.

### Will AI browsers replace HR jobs?

No, AI browsers do not replace you. They [handle boring tasks](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-agentic-workflows-productivity-automation/) so you can focus on people. You still make the big decisions and bring the human touch.

### How do I start using an AI browser for HR work?

Start small. Pick one task, like scheduling or resume screening. Try the AI browser for that job. Watch how it works. If you like it, add more tasks over time.

### What if the AI browser makes a mistake?

You can always check the AI’s work. Most AI browsers let you review and fix errors. Stay involved and give feedback. This helps the AI learn and get better.
