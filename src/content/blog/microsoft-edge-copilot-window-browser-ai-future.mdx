## Microsoft Edge as a Window Browser

### Copilot Mode Overview

When you use Microsoft Edge as a window browser, you unlock Copilot Mode. This mode changes how you interact with the browser. Copilot Mode brings a set of smart features that help you manage your browsing tasks with ease. You do not need to switch between different tools or tabs. Instead, you find everything in one place.

Here is what you get with [Copilot Mode in Microsoft Edge](https://blogs.windows.com/msedgedev/2025/07/28/introducing-copilot-mode-in-edge-a-new-way-to-browse-the-web/):

*   [Multi-tab context awareness](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/microsoft-adds-copilot-mode-to-edge-with-multi-tab-ai-analysis/552441/) lets Copilot understand and compare information across all your open tabs. You can make decisions faster because Copilot sees the bigger picture.
    
*   A unified input interface combines chat, search, and navigation. You type or speak your question in one box, and Copilot responds right away.
    
*   Voice navigation allows you to use spoken commands to open tabs or find information. This hands-free option makes browsing more accessible.
    
*   [Real-time summarization](https://thetechthinker.com/microsoft-edge-copilot-mode-future-browsing/) gives you quick summaries of web pages, articles, PDFs, and even videos. You save time by reading only the most important points.
    
*   Smart Q&A interaction means Copilot answers your questions based on what you are viewing. You get context-aware help without searching elsewhere.
    
*   AI-powered content creation helps you draft emails, social posts, or other writing tasks. Copilot suggests words and phrases to improve your work.
    
*   Personalized suggestions appear as Copilot learns your habits. You see shortcuts and tips that match your browsing style.
    
*   The dynamic assistance pane sits on the side of your screen. You interact with Copilot without losing sight of your current page.
    
*   Topic-based journeys organize your browsing sessions into groups. Copilot suggests next steps to keep you focused on your goals.
    
*   Privacy and user control remain a priority. You decide what data Copilot can access, and you see clear cues when Copilot is active.
    

> Tip: You can turn Copilot Mode on or off at any time. This gives you the choice between a classic browsing experience and an AI-powered one.

With Copilot Mode, Microsoft Edge becomes more than just a browser. It acts as your assistant, helping you complete tasks, find answers, and stay organized.

You may also face other drawbacks:
