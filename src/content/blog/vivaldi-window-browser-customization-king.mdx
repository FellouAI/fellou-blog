Here are the [most popular extensions Vivaldi users choose in 2025](https://forum.vivaldi.net/topic/100674/found-this-checking-on-my-extension-see-post):

1.  **AutoplayStopper** – Stops videos from playing automatically.
    
2.  **Bulky close tabs** – Helps you manage and close many tabs at once.
    
3.  **Cookie AutoDelete** – Deletes cookies when you close tabs.
    
4.  **Cookie Notice Blocker** – Blocks cookie consent pop-ups.
    
5.  **Decentraleyes** – Improves privacy by serving local resources.
    
6.  **Disconnect** – Blocks trackers and protects your privacy.
    
7.  **Hard Refresh** – Lets you refresh pages completely.
    
8.  **Open in new tab** – Opens links in new tabs for easier browsing.
    
9.  **Stylus** – Lets you apply custom styles to websites.
    
10.  **uBlock Origin** – Blocks ads and improves security.
     

You can also find alternatives like Privacy Tweaks, Stylebot, and userscript managers such as Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey. These tools help you customize your browsing even more. The Vivaldi community often shares guides and scripts to help you get the most out of these extensions.
