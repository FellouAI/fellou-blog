## FAQ

### What is a Chrome automation extension?

A Chrome automation extension is a tool you add to your browser. It helps you do tasks faster. You can write, organize, or manage meetings with less effort. These tools use AI to save you time.

### Are these extensions safe to use?

Most popular extensions are safe. You should always check reviews and privacy policies before installing. Stick to tools from trusted sources. Update your extensions often to keep your data secure.

### Can I use more than one extension at a time?

Yes, you can! Many people use several extensions together. Just make sure your browser does not slow down. Try different combinations to see what works best for you.

### Do I need to pay for these extensions?

Many extensions offer free versions. Some have paid plans with extra features. You can start with the free option and upgrade if you need more tools.

### Will these tools work on other browsers?

Most extensions work best on Chrome. Some also support browsers like Edge, Brave, or Firefox. Check the extension’s page to see where it works.
