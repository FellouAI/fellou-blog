## Compliance and AI

### Regulatory Support

You face strict rules when you use ai in consulting. Two main frameworks shape your grc strategy: [GDPR and HIPAA](https://www.ailoitte.com/blog/hippa-vs-gdpr/). GDPR covers all personal data from EU citizens, no matter where your business is. HIPAA protects health information in the U.S. Both demand strong grc controls, like [encryption, access management, and clear breach notifications](https://www.mooglelabs.com/gdpr-and-hipaa-compliance). GDPR gives people more rights over their data, while HIPAA focuses on patient health records. If you work with clients in healthcare or across borders, you must follow both. These rules require you to set up grc processes that keep ai systems safe and transparent.

> Note: You must always check which grc rules apply to your clients and projects. This helps you avoid fines and build trust.

### Compliance Setup

You can set up your ai browser for compliance by following a clear process. Start by [reviewing your ai systems and data practices](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/cybersecurity-risk-regulatory/library/tech-regulatory-policy-developments/privacy-laws.html) to match grc requirements. Update your privacy policies to explain how ai handles data and makes decisions. Add consent and opt-out tools, so users control their data. Strengthen your grc controls with encryption and access checks. Build a baseline for grc compliance to manage ai risks. Stay alert to new grc laws and adapt your setup as needed.

[Follow these steps for a strong compliance setup](https://www.smarsh.com/blog/thought-leadership/managing-ai-to-ensure-compliance-with-data-privacy-laws):

1.  Understand the grc rules in each place you work.
    
2.  Run regular risk checks and impact assessments for your ai systems.
    
3.  Use privacy by design, making sure ai protects data from the start.
    
4.  Test and audit your [ai browser](https://fellou.ai/blog/ai-browser-fellou-vs-perplexity-comet-comparison-review/) often to keep grc standards high.
    
5.  Tell users how ai works and what data it uses.
    
6.  Respect user choices about ai data use.
    
7.  Give users ways to see, change, or delete their data.
    
8.  Keep records to show your grc compliance.
