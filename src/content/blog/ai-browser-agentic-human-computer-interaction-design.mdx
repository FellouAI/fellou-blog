## Core Principles of Delegation

In e-commerce, fast feedback helps you track your orders or payments. The agents update you on each step. You never feel out of the loop. This approach supports consumer trust and makes your experience smooth.

You may notice that ai can sometimes take over too much in commerce. When this happens, you might feel like you lose control. In e-commerce, you want to stay in charge of your shopping and choices. [Fellou Davinci](https://fellou.ai/) helps you avoid over-automation by letting you step in at any time. You can pause, edit, or stop what the agents do. The visual workflow shows you every step. > Tip: Always check the workflow before you let ai finish a task. This habit helps you keep control and enjoy a better commerce experience.

### Ambiguity

Sometimes, ai may not understand what you want in commerce. Ambiguity can cause mistakes in e-commerce, like buying the wrong item or missing a deal. Fellou Davinci solves this by asking you clear questions. The [agents](https://fellou.ai/eko/docs/agents/) break down your goals into simple steps. You see each step and can change it if needed. This process removes confusion and helps you get the results you want. In commerce, clear intent leads to better shopping and fewer errors.

You can use natural language to talk to ai. If the system is not sure, it will ask for more details. This makes your commerce experience smooth and easy to follow.

### Maintaining Trust

Trust is key in commerce, especially when you share personal data. You want to know that your data privacy is safe. Fellou Davinci uses privacy mode to protect your information. You can turn off ai tracking when you need privacy. The system also uses efficiency visualization to show you how much time ai saves you in commerce. This builds trust and shows the value of using ai.

To keep your trust, the system uses [several best practices](https://medium.com/%40jayamohanmohanan/achieving-autonomy-ai-agents-to-context-aware-agentic-ai-7272efe73161):

1.  You always have human oversight. You can monitor and change what ai does in commerce.
    
2.  The system uses strong security to protect data privacy. It follows rules like [GDPR and CCPA](https://fellou.ai/policy).
    
3.  You see clear explanations for each ai decision. This helps you understand and trust the process.
    
4.  The system keeps logs and audits actions to spot problems early.
    
5.  You control who can access your data privacy settings.
    

> Note: Always use privacy mode when you want extra data privacy in e-commerce. This feature helps you feel safe and builds consumer trust.
