## Privacy & Performance
Fellou creates virtual workspaces locally, running tasks in hidden windows that don’t interfere with your current browsing.

Logins always stay on your own device. Security remains in your hands.

That’s why we call it the first truly <strong>Agentic Browser</strong> — it doesn’t just layer AI on top, it <strong>redefines the browser itself as the Agent’s workspace.</strong>
