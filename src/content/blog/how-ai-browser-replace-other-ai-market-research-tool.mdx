## Limitations

### Scenarios

AI Browser uses automation and agentic workflows for market research. But there are times when it cannot do everything that Fellou, Quantilope, or GWI Spark can do.

*   Some companies need a strong system that works well with other services. AI browsers sometimes do not have these features, so they cannot [do every job](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3359812840802304).
    
*   New AI browsers might have problems with speed, trust, or getting things right. These problems can make data analysis and insights less good.
    
*   Keeping data safe and private is very important. Companies with private data must follow privacy laws and rules.
    
*   Adding new features too fast can make some parts not work well. Some hard workflows or special insights might be better on older, proven platforms.
    
*   Platforms like Quantilope use both people and AI tools together. [AI browsers alone may not do as well](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-disruption-professional-services-strategic-analysis-david-pereira-pfk5f) when projects need deep data checks or special research.
    

> Note: If a business needs hard data jobs, special insights, or strong connections, it may still need special platforms or a mix of tools.
