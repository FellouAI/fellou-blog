### Limitations and Risks

**AI** is strong, but there are some risks. Sometimes, **ai** can be unfair because of the data or how it is made. This can change your research results. Privacy and data safety are very important. **AI** tools use a lot of data, and leaks can happen. **AI** does not always understand the whole market or people’s feelings, so your analysis might miss things. If you only use **ai**, you might stop thinking for yourself. Automated **ai** actions, like trading bots, can even cause sudden market changes.

**AI** in research has other limits, like spreading wrong information or using data in a bad way. You should always check your **ai** results and use human judgment too.
