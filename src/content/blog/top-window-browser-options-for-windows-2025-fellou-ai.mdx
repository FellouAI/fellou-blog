## 8\. Tor Browser

### Overview

You want the best for security and anonymity. Tor Browser stands out as the top choice for users who need to stay hidden online. This browser routes your traffic through a network of servers, called nodes, to hide your IP address. You can visit both regular websites and special .onion sites for extra privacy. Many journalists, activists, and privacy-focused users pick Tor Browser when they need the best for security.

### Features

Tor Browser gives you strong tools to protect your identity:

1.  [Routes only your browser traffic through the Tor network](https://ssd.eff.org/module/how-use-tor-windows), keeping other apps separate.
    
2.  Disables plugins and [warns you not to install extra extensions](https://security.stackexchange.com/questions/95087/using-tor-browser-on-a-windows-machine), which could leak your data.
    
3.  Lets you access .onion sites, which use end-to-end encryption and hidden addresses.
    
4.  Blocks trackers and fingerprinting by making all users look the same.
    
5.  Advises you not to open downloaded files while online, to avoid revealing your real IP address.
    
6.  Offers a support page with tips to help you stay anonymous.
    

> Note: You should avoid logging into personal accounts or revealing personal details. Your behavior matters for your privacy.

### Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- |
| Best for security and anonymity | Slower page loads (10+ seconds for some sites) |
| Access to .onion and regular sites | Many sites block Tor traffic (like Netflix, some banks) |
| Blocks trackers and fingerprinting | Not for streaming or online banking |
| Free and open source | Needs careful use to keep you anonymous |

### Best Browser for Anonymity

You want the best for security and privacy. Tor Browser gives you the highest level of anonymity by [routing your browsing through multiple encrypted layers](https://usa.kaspersky.com/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-tor-browser-and-anonymity/30649/). You get protection from trackers, fingerprinting, and surveillance. If you need privacy-first web browsing or you are an activist, Tor Browser is the best for security and the top browser for anonymity on Windows.
